# 🧾 Invoice Generator v3.0

A clean, cross-platform desktop app for generating professional PDF invoices with custom branding, payment options, and email support.

---

## ⚠️ Windows SmartScreen Warning

Windows may show a "Windows protected your PC" warning when you run the app.
This is because the app is not signed with a paid certificate.

**How to run it:**
1. Click "More info"
2. Click "Run anyway"
3. The app will open normally

The app is safe — you can verify the code right here!


---


## 🚀 Features

- **📄 Professional PDF Invoices** — Generate clean, branded invoices instantly
- **✏️ Custom Invoice Names** — Name your invoices (e.g., "INV-001", "Project Alpha")
- **📧 Email Support** — Send invoices directly from the app via SMTP (Gmail, Outlook, etc.)
- **🏦 Payment Options** — Configure Bank Transfer and BPay details
- **🌙 Light / Dark Theme** — Toggle between light and dark mode (saved across sessions)
- **🏢 Business Settings** — Configure your company name, email, and phone
- **📦 Cross-Platform** — Available for Windows, macOS, and Linux

## ⚙️ Setup Instructions

### First Run
1. Open the app
2. Go to the **Business & SMTP** tab
3. Enter your business details and email settings
4. Go to the **Payment** tab
5. Enter your bank and BPay details
6. Start creating invoices! 🎉

### Email Setup (SMTP)
- **Gmail:** Enable "App Passwords" in your Google Account settings
- **Outlook/Office 365:** Use your email password or app password
- **Other providers:** Check your provider's SMTP settings
